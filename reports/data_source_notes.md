# Data Source and Coverage Notes

**Dataset:** e-CHARAK Price Wise medicinal plant market-price reports  
**Study period:** 2017–2025  
**Source:** https://echarak.ayush.gov.in/Pricewise

## Source Coverage Limitation

The official e-CHARAK portal provides the reporting periods available in the downloaded source files. Some months and fortnights are absent from the collected dataset. These gaps reflect the coverage of the source data supplied for this study and must not be interpreted as months in which market prices did not exist.

## Data Handling Policy

- Preserve the original Excel files.
- Identify available and missing reporting periods.
- Do not invent prices or create artificial observations for unavailable periods.
- Apply the project's documented fortnight-label convention.
- Disclose coverage limitations in the analysis, report, and Power BI dashboard.

## Validation Status

The exact period coverage, price definitions, units, and worksheet anomalies remain subject to further validation.
